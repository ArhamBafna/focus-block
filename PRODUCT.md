# Product

## Register

product

## Users

General public: freelancers, students, and employees who need to protect focused time from distracting sites and apps.

## Product Purpose

FocusBlock helps people start, maintain, and review distraction-free focus sessions. Success means users can block distractions quickly, trust the controls, and return to work with less stress.

## Brand Personality

Firm, focused, de-stressing. Calm enough to reduce pressure; decisive enough to make focus boundaries feel reliable.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy gamification, guilt-heavy productivity UI, generic SaaS dashboards, decorative animations, and controls that feel unclear or weak.

## Design Principles

- Make focus boundaries feel decisive and trustworthy.
- Keep each action easy to understand at a glance.
- Use calm visual rhythm to lower cognitive load.
- Give direct feedback for every meaningful action.
- Reserve visual emphasis for the user’s next decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, keyboard-visible focus, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Default experience may use purposeful motion; users who request reduced motion receive an immediate alternative.
